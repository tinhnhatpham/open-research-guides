How Each Investigation Is Built

Every project starts with a practical decision ordinary households face, then checks what free tools already exist so we target real gaps where people otherwise hit clunky agency portals, paywalls, or affiliate SEO copy.

We verify claims against primary federal bulk filings, regulatory dockets, and peer-reviewed studies, then publish both the interactive tool and the underlying cleaning scripts openly on GitHub.
