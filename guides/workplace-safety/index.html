5. Data Forensics, Honest Caveats & Open Dataset Downloads

Critical Methodological Caveats (Read Before Citing):
  • 29 Federal OSHA Jurisdictions vs. 21 State-Plan States (SIR): OSHA's 2025 Form 300A dataset covers all 50 states, but Federal OSHA's Severe Injury Reports (SIR) cover only the 29 Federal OSHA jurisdictions (plus federal agencies like USPS). Private employers in 21 State-Plan states (including CA, WA, OR, MI, MN, NC, VA, IN, TN, AZ, MD) report severe injuries to state agencies, not Federal SIR—so national employers heavily concentrated in CA/WA/MI have undercounted SIR totals here.
  • 3,837 Exact Duplicate Filings Caught & Removed: Raw ITA_300A_Summary_Data_2025 contains 400,288 rows, including thousands of duplicate submissions filed on consecutive days under different establishment_IDs (e.g., Costco submitted 1,513 rows for 731 sites; H-E-B submitted 894 rows for 459 sites with extra whitespace). We deduplicated on normalized street address + ZIP5 + state + employees + hours + injuries (retaining the latest filing) and removed 19,036 clerical hours/size outliers.
  • Reporting Coverage & Underreporting: Electronic Form 300A submission is mandatory for establishments with ≥250 workers (or 20–249 workers in designated high-hazard industries), not tiny low-hazard offices. Furthermore, academic studies and OSHA audits show some employers under-record injuries or shift cases to "light duty" (DJTR) rather than days away (DAFW)—which is why we highlight DART (both combined) alongside SIR amputations.
