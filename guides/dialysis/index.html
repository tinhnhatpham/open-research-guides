3. Five Verified Findings (Including What Surprised Us)

1. The Two-Company Dialysis Duopoly (73.5%)

89.5% (6,689) of U.S. dialysis clinics are for-profit. Just two corporations—DaVita (2,801 clinics, 37.5%) and Fresenius Medical Care (2,688 clinics, 36.0%)—operate nearly three out of four clinics nationwide. In many counties, patients have no non-duopoly clinic nearby.

2. The Kidney Transplant Waitlist Gap

A kidney transplant is the gold-standard treatment for kidney failure, yet waitlisting varies dramatically by clinic. Non-profit clinics waitlist 19.3% of prevalent patients vs. 16.3% at for-profits (Fresenius: 15.7%, DaVita: 16.8%). Integrated non-profit Kaiser Permanente achieves 41.9% waitlisted and 100% home-dialysis availability.

3. Honest Plot Twist: Small Independent For-Profits Perform Worst

Contrary to simplistic "big chain = always worst" narratives, Independent For-Profit clinics (n=449) post the worst national averages: 2.04★, 24.2 mortality per 100 patient-years, and 28.1% long-term catheter use (vs. 17.3% at DaVita and 19.2% at Fresenius). Non-profits also show higher raw infection SIR (0.57 vs 0.30), partly reflecting sicker hospital-based acute starts. Always evaluate the specific clinic, not just the brand logo.

4. The Long-Term Catheter Risk (39% of Clinics ≥ 20%)

Nephrology guidelines strongly favor an arteriovenous (AV) fistula over a long-term central venous catheter, which carries much higher bloodstream infection and death risk. Yet 2,915 clinics (39.0%) still have 20% or more of adult patients on long-term catheters.

5. Working-Age Access Gap: Only 15.2% Offer Evening Shifts

In-center hemodialysis takes ~4 hours, three days a week. Only 1,136 clinics (15.2%) offer late/evening shifts—forcing many working-age adults out of jobs unless they can switch to Peritoneal Dialysis (PD) or Home Hemodialysis (offered by 56.6% of clinics). Use the Evening / Late Shift and Offers Home Dialysis filter chips above to find compatible clinics in your ZIP code.

5. Practical Patient Rights & Open Data Downloads

Five rights every dialysis patient & caregiver should know: (1) You can self-refer directly to a kidney transplant center (check wait times at SRTR.org) without waiting on your clinic; (2) Ask your nephrologist early about switching from a catheter to an AV fistula; (3) Ask whether Home Peritoneal Dialysis (PD) or Home Hemodialysis fits your lifestyle and work schedule; (4) You have the right to switch dialysis clinics if your current facility has poor outcomes or staffing; (5) Every clinic must post its ESRD Network grievance phone number if you face unsafe care or pressure.
