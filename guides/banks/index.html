5. Four Verified Findings & Honest Banking Nuances

1. The $38B/Year "Big 4" Deposit Skim

JPMorgan Chase ($2.17T), Bank of America ($1.96T), Wells Fargo ($1.48T), and Citibank ($832B) hold 35.7% ($6.44T) of all U.S. domestic deposits—yet paid a weighted average of just 1.71% in FY2025. Even compared against the 75th-percentile U.S. bank (2.30%), that gap equals $38.0 billion/year (and well over $100B/year compared to 3.8%–4.1% FDIC-insured online banks).

2. $34.38 Billion in Deposit Fees — 65% From 16 Megabanks

Just 16 Megabanks collected $22.37B in FY2025 deposit service charges (ISERCHG): JPMorgan Chase ($6.28B), Wells Fargo ($4.65B), Bank of America ($3.92B), and Citibank ($1.32B). Meanwhile, high-yield FDIC-insured online banks like Synchrony (4.10%), Sallie Mae (3.92%), Barclays (3.84%), and Goldman Sachs Bank USA (3.49%) collected $0.0M in deposit service charges.

3. Both Blended & Interest-Bearing Rates Tell the Story

Megabanks often argue their blended deposit rate looks low because they run huge networks of 0%-interest checking accounts. That's partly true (JPMorgan holds 27.1% non-interest deposits)—which is why we report BOTH Blended Rate AND Interest-Bearing-Only Rate (EDEPDOM / DEPIDOM). Even on interest-bearing deposits alone, Chase (2.19%), BofA (2.17%), and Wells Fargo (2.06%) trail top online FDIC banks by 1.7–2.0 percentage points.

4. Uninsured Deposit Exposure & The $250k Rule

After the 2023 SVB and First Republic runs, uninsured deposits matter. Megabanks average 45.2% uninsured domestic deposits (Citibank 79.7%, Chase 58.7%, U.S. Bank 52.2%) vs. 22.5% at community banks and 6%–11% at retail online banks like Synchrony and Sallie Mae. For everyday savers under $250,000, FDIC protection is identical across every bank in this table.

  1. FDIC BankFind API v2 (api.fdic.gov/banks): Active Institutions (4,204 banks) joined to full-year FY2025 (20251231) and pre-hike FY2021 (20211231) Call Reports.
  2. Exact Call Report Formulas: Blended Effective Deposit Rate = EDEPDOM / DEPDOM; Interest-Bearing Deposit Rate = EDEPDOM / DEPIDOM; Deposit Service Charges = ISERCHG (explicitly distinct from ISC, which is Interest on Securities); Pass-Through ("Deposit Beta") = (DepRate2025 − DepRate2021) / (AssetYield2025 − AssetYield2021) for banks with ≥+0.50% asset yield gain; Uninsured Share = DEPUNINS / DEPDOM.
  3. Scope Note: Covers FDIC-insured commercial banks and savings institutions (credit unions are regulated separately by the NCUA). Effective rates reflect actual interest paid across all domestic accounts (checking, savings, money market, CDs) rather than promotional teaser APYs.
