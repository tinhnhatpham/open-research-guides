The Credit Union Reality Check: All 4,363 U.S. Credit Unions vs. Big Banks (NCUA 2021–2025)

Zero-affiliate audit of all 4,363 federally insured U.S. credit unions (145.9M members, $2.46T assets, Q4 2021 & Q4 2025 NCUA 5300 Call Reports) benchmarked directly against our 4,204 FDIC Bank audit • Updated November 2026

The Real Verdict (Testing the "Just Switch to a Credit Union" Advice): Federal Call Reports tell a far sharper story than internet slogans. On savings yield, credit unions beat the Big 4 Megabanks (1.86% vs 1.71%) yet actually lag overall FDIC banks (1.99% wavg, 1.85% median vs CU 1.24% median) and top zero-fee online banks (3.5%–4.1%). Where credit unions truly crush banks is borrowing costs (median Credit Card APR 11.57%, New Auto 5.50%) and deposit safety (just 3.7% median uninsured shares vs 45.2% at Megabanks).

4. Accounting Notes & Open Dataset Downloads

Verified NCUA 5300 Account Mapping: Deposit rate paid = (ACCT_380 Dividends + ACCT_381 Interest on Deposits) / ACCT_018 Total Shares & Deposits; Fee Income = ACCT_131 (all member fee income including NSF/overdraft and loan/account fees); Uninsured Shares = ACCT_068A / ACCT_018; Loan APRs = ACCT_521 (Credit Card), ACCT_523 (New Auto), and ACCT_524 (Used Auto) divided by 100.
