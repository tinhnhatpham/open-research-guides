1. The "5-Star Illusion": What Even Top-Rated Facilities Hide

Across the 3,154 U.S. nursing homes that currently hold CMS's highest 5-star overall rating, payroll-verified PBJ records and enforcement files still uncover major warning signs:

Even among 5-star nursing homes: 23.7% slash total nursing hours by 15% or more on weekends • 14.9% still suffer 50%+ annual nursing turnover • 14.6% have active federal fines on record. Never stop at the headline star score.

2. Ownership Matters: Non-Profit vs. Government vs. For-Profit

Roughly three out of four U.S. nursing homes (74.1%) are for-profit. On average, non-profit facilities deliver 44 more minutes of nursing care per resident every single day, have lower turnover, and receive roughly half the average federal fine.

4. Six Concrete Checks for Families Touring a Facility

  1. Visit unannounced on a Saturday or Sunday afternoon: Nationally, Registered Nurse (RN) hours plunge 30.4% on weekends. Seeing a facility on Sunday shows its real baseline.
  2. Look up the 6 hidden metrics on medicare.gov/care-compare: Check (1) Ownership type, (2) Weekend total & RN hours, (3) Total nursing turnover, (4) RN turnover, (5) Administrator turnover, and (6) Federal fines / abuse icon.
  3. Ask directly about agency/temp nurse share: High turnover (>50%) means staff do not know your parent's medications, fall triggers, or dietary needs.
  4. Check if the facility changed ownership in the last 12 months: Rapid buyouts often precede staffing cuts or administrator departures.
  5. Read the actual inspection narratives (Form 2567): A single "Immediate Jeopardy" citation tells you far more than a star rating average.
  6. Compare against your state average above: Use the table to see whether a home is actually outperforming local peers—not just riding a lenient regional curve.

5. Primary Sources & Open Reproducibility

  1. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): Provider Data Catalog — Nursing Home Provider Information (Dataset 4pq5-n9py, 14,686 certified facilities, Payroll-Based Journal verified staffing & federal penalty records).
  2. Reproducible Code: Full Python script (data/nursing-homes/analyze_cms_nursing_homes.py) and dataset (summary.json) are open in the repository so anyone can re-run and verify every state and ownership statistic.
  3. Data Caveat: State averages mask wide facility-to-facility variation—there are excellent for-profit homes and struggling non-profits. Always check the specific facility's PBJ numbers and inspection reports on Medicare Care Compare.
