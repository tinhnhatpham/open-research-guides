4. Honest Methodological Caveats & Open Dataset Downloads

Important Caveats (Read Before Citing):
  • NWS Field Estimates vs. Final Insured Losses: NOAA Storm Events records initial field-office damage estimates (`DAMAGE_PROPERTY`). While exact for comparing relative county hazards (flood vs. hail vs. tornado), field estimates systematically understate final insured catastrophe losses for mega-hurricanes and urban wildfires compared to NOAA's separate Billion-Dollar Disasters tally.
  • County FIPS vs. NWS Forecast Zones (`CZ_TYPE=C` vs `Z`): NOAA records tornadoes, hail, flash floods, and severe winds by County FIPS, but records hurricanes, wildfires, heat waves, and winter storms by NWS Forecast Zone. Our national & state tables include 100% of all 687,227 events; our county lookup merges all County-FIPS rows plus 165,474 exact-name 1:1 zone-to-county matches, while multi-county mountain/coastal zones remain in state totals.
  • Always Check Parcel-Level Flood Maps: County-level history does not replace parcel elevation—always verify exact flood zones at FEMA Flood Map Service Center and pair with our Guide #07 Flood Claims Reality.
