5. Honest Methodological Caveats & Open Dataset Downloads

Important Caveats (Read Before Deciding):
  • Major Matters Huge: Institution-wide earnings reflect what students major in (engineering/nursing-heavy schools like Georgia Tech post $100k+ medians; arts/music schools post lower early-career salaries). Always check program-level earnings for your intended major on the official College Scorecard.
  • Parent PLUS Debt Applies Only to PLUS Borrowers: Federal Parent PLUS medians reflect families who took out Parent PLUS loans—not every student on campus—and carry uncapped borrowing limits up to the full cost of attendance, making them the biggest hidden family-retirement debt trap in U.S. higher education.
  • Title IV Cohort Scope: Scorecard earnings (`MD_EARN_WNE_P10`) cover former federal financial-aid recipients who are working and not enrolled 10 years after entry, and GT_THRESHOLD_P10 measures the share earning more than a typical high school graduate (~$32k).
